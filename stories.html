Stories

Real situations from our home, told with care. Names and details are always changed to protect every child.

Our stories

Behind every child is a story of hope

Pick one and read it in a minute. Each story is based on the real situations of children who have come through our doors.

Protecting every child

We never share real names, photos or details that could identify a child. Our stories are drawn from real situations, with details changed and, sometimes, several children’s experiences woven into one. The pictures are AI-generated or illustrated, and none shows a real child.

Her First Day of School

She arrived as a young girl who had never sat in a classroom. Years later, she stood at the front of one.

Where it began

She came to us as a young girl, an orphan who had never been to school. The first weeks were hard: unfamiliar letters, strange numbers and a classroom full of noisy children.

What changed

Her caregivers enrolled her in school right away and sat beside her over homework every evening. Slowly, her fear turned into curiosity, and she finished secondary school with strong results.

Today

She went on to earn a diploma in early childhood education. Today she is a preschool teacher overseas, helping little ones take their very first steps into learning.

With the right support, every child can rise above where they began.

她的第一天上学

来到小屋时她还是个小女孩，从没踏进过教室。多年以后，她站在了讲台上。

故事的起点

她来到我们这里时还是个小女孩，是个从未上过学的孤儿。最初几周很不容易：陌生的字母、陌生的数字，还有满是吵闹孩子的教室。

改变发生了

看护们立刻帮她办理入学，每天晚上陪在她身边写功课。慢慢地，她的害怕变成了好奇，中学毕业时成绩优异。

如今

后来，她取得了幼儿教育文凭。如今她在海外担任幼儿园老师，陪伴小朋友迈出学习的第一步。

只要得到适当的支持，每个孩子都能超越自己的起点。

She Had No One to Take Her In

After losing both parents, there was no relative who could give her a home. Then a door opened.

Where it began

She lost both of her parents to illness while she was still growing up. Her only sister was living with relatives, and no one in the family was able to offer her a stable home of her own.

Coming home

At our home she found what every child needs after a loss: a bed of her own, meals shared around a table, and caregivers who notice when she is quiet and sit down beside her.

What comes next

Grief does not simply disappear. But with safety, school and people who care, a girl who felt alone can start to feel that she belongs again.

Every child deserves a place to call home.

没有人能收留她

失去双亲后，亲人中没有谁能给她一个家。后来，一扇门为她打开了。

故事的起点

她在成长的岁月里失去了双亲。唯一的姐姐寄住在亲戚家，家里也没有人能给她一个稳定的家。

来到小屋

在小屋里，她得到了每个孩子在失去亲人后最需要的东西：属于自己的床铺、围坐一桌的饭菜，以及会在她沉默时坐到她身边的看护。

接下来

悲伤不会凭空消失。但有了安全的环境、学业和关心她的人，一个曾经感到孤单的女孩，会慢慢重新找到归属感。

每个孩子都值得拥有一个可以称之为家的地方。

Where He Belongs

He lost his mother as a young boy and spent years searching for a place to belong.

Where it began

He was still a young boy when his mother passed away. His father was too unwell to care for him, so a relative took him in. He was fed and sheltered, but he was lonely, and the loneliness came out as anger.

What changed

When he joined our home, we saw the anger for what it was: a plea for love. Kind caregivers and new friends slowly earned his trust, and his schoolwork began to improve.

Today

He went on to study at a university overseas. Today he shows younger children that where you start does not decide where you finish.

Where you begin does not decide where you end.

他的归属

年幼丧母的他，花了好些年寻找一个归属之地。

故事的起点

他还是个小男孩时，母亲就去世了。父亲身体不好，无法照顾他，只好由亲戚收留。他有饭吃、有地方住，却很孤单，孤单化成了愤怒。

改变发生了

来到小屋后，我们明白那份愤怒其实是对爱的呼唤。温暖的看护和新朋友渐渐赢得了他的信任，他的成绩也开始进步。

如今

后来，他考上了海外的大学。如今，他用自己的经历告诉年幼的孩子：起点并不能决定终点。

起点，并不能决定终点。

Grandma Did Her Best

She grew up on her grandmother’s love, until her grandmother could no longer carry the load.

Where it began

Her grandmother raised her from the very beginning and was her whole world: bedtime stories, home-cooked meals, a hand to hold. Then her grandmother’s health began to fail, and caring for a growing child became too much.

Coming home

After a short stay with another relative, she came to us. The first nights were tearful. Our caregivers kept the routines gentle, the lights low, and a seat ready for her at the table.

What comes next

She is learning that love can come from more than one place, and that she can keep growing with confidence. Her grandmother’s love is still a part of her.

Love can come from more than one place.

外婆已经尽力了

她在外婆的疼爱中长大，直到外婆再也扛不起这份重担。

故事的起点

外婆从一开始就把她带大，是她的全部世界：睡前故事、热腾腾的家常饭、一双牵着她的手。后来，外婆的身体渐渐衰弱，照顾一个成长中的孩子变得力不从心。

来到小屋

在亲戚家短暂住过之后，她来到了小屋。最初的几个夜晚，她常常哭泣。看护们让作息温柔平稳，把灯光调暗，并在餐桌旁为她留好一个位子。

接下来

她正在学会：爱可以来自不止一个地方，她也能充满自信地继续成长。外婆给她的爱，依然是她的一部分。

爱，可以来自不止一个地方。

Two Siblings, One Promise

They had already lost so much. The one thing they refused to lose was each other.

Where it began

Two siblings lost both of their parents far too young. Suddenly the people who should have comforted them were gone, and all they had left was each other.

Coming home

We made them a promise: they would stay together. They share a roof, walk to school side by side, and the elder keeps a quiet eye on the younger at every meal.

What comes next

With steady care, counselling and a gentle routine, they are healing together. Knowing they are not alone is half of the journey.

Nobody should have to heal alone.

两个手足，一个承诺

他们已经失去太多，唯一不愿失去的，是彼此。

故事的起点

两个手足过早地失去了双亲。本该安慰他们的人突然不在了，他们只剩下彼此。

来到小屋

我们许下一个承诺：让他们待在一起。他们住在同一个屋檐下，并肩走路上学，每顿饭，年长的那个都会默默照看着年幼的。

接下来

在稳定的照顾、辅导与温和的作息中，他们一起慢慢疗愈。知道自己并不孤单，已经是旅程的一半。

没有人应该独自疗伤。

The Boy Who Rewrote His Story

As a young boy he had never had a full meal or a day of school. Now he comes home with awards.

Where it began

As a young boy he had never had a full meal and had never been to school. His days were long, tiring and frightening, and he had no idea that a different life was possible.

What changed

After long efforts together with the Welfare Department, he was brought to safety and into our home. He started school far behind his classmates, but he never stopped trying.

Today

Today he has won a class excellence award, a writing prize and a medal in a spelling competition. His story proves that no child’s future is decided by where he started.

Given the chance, every child can shine.

改写自己故事的男孩

年幼的他，没吃过一顿饱饭，也没上过一天学。如今，他捧着奖状回家。

故事的起点

年幼的他，从未吃过一顿饱饭，也从未上过学。日子漫长、疲惫又惊恐，他甚至不知道，原来还有另一种生活。

改变发生了

在与福利部门长久的共同努力下，他终于被带到安全的地方，来到了小屋。他上学时远远落后于同学，但从未放弃。

如今

如今，他赢得了班级优秀奖、写作奖，还在拼字比赛中获得奖牌。他的故事证明：孩子的未来，不由起点决定。

只要有机会，每个孩子都能发光。

The Family That Lost Its Provider

When the only person earning for the family was gone, the question was not only grief. It was: how will we live?

Where it began

Her father worked long, hard days to keep the family going. After years of exhaustion, his health gave way and he passed away, taking with him the family’s only income and the person they loved most.

Coming home

Behind the grief was a heavy worry about the next meal and the next school fee. Our home gave the children a safe place to be children again.

What comes next

With steady support for her schooling, she is building the skills to stand on her own, one step at a time.

Education is the key to breaking the cycle of poverty.

失去顶梁柱的家

当家里唯一的收入来源离开，面对的不只是悲伤，还有：我们该怎么生活？

故事的起点

父亲日复一日辛苦工作，撑起整个家。多年的劳累之后，他的身体垮了，离开了人世，也带走了家里唯一的收入，和他们最爱的人。

来到小屋

悲伤背后，是对下一餐饭、下一笔学费的沉重担忧。小屋给了孩子们一个安全的地方，让他们重新做回孩子。

接下来

在学业上得到持续的支持，她正一步一步培养自立的能力。

教育是打破贫困循环的关键。

Too Many Doors, No Home

Kind relatives took her in, one after another. But kindness alone could not pay the bills.

Where it began

After her family fell apart, she stayed with one relative, then another, then a third. Each of them helped for as long as they could. Each of them was struggling too.

Coming home

Moving from house to house meant new rooms, new rules and never quite unpacking her bag. When no steady arrangement was left, she came to us, hoping for something that would last.

What comes next

At our home, her bag is finally unpacked. A steady bed, a daily routine and a school uniform ready for the morning: small things that add up to a big sense of security.

Stability is a gift a child can feel.

门太多，却没有一个家

亲戚们一个接一个收留她。但光有善意，付不起生活的开销。

故事的起点

家庭破裂之后，她先住在一位亲戚家，接着是另一位，再来是第三位。每一位都尽力帮忙，但每一位自己也很吃力。

来到小屋

不断搬家，意味着新的房间、新的规矩，和永远没有真正打开过的行李。当再也找不到稳定的安排时，她来到了小屋，盼望有一个能长久的家。

接下来

在小屋里，她的行李终于打开了。一张固定的床、规律的日常、明早就能穿上的校服：这些小事，汇成了一份踏实的安全感。

稳定，是孩子能感受到的礼物。

A Safe Place to Sleep

Sometimes the bravest thing a family can do is admit that a child needs somewhere safer.

Where it began

When a child’s home can no longer keep them safe, the Welfare Department can step in. Some children arrive quietly, holding a small bag, not sure what to expect.

Coming home

We begin with the basics: a warm bed, a hot meal and a gentle hello. Trust takes time, so we never rush it. Every child sets their own pace.

What comes next

Safe nights lead to calmer days, and calmer days lead to learning, laughing and trusting again. That is what a safe place can do.

Every child deserves to feel safe.

一个安心入睡的地方

有时候，家人最勇敢的决定，是承认孩子需要一个更安全的地方。

故事的起点

当孩子的家再也无法保护他们时，福利部门可以介入。有些孩子悄悄地来，手里提着一个小包，不知道将会遇见什么。

来到小屋

我们从最基本的做起：一张温暖的床、一顿热饭，和一声轻轻的问候。信任需要时间，所以我们从不催促，每个孩子按自己的步伐来。

接下来

安稳的夜晚带来平静的白天，平静的白天带来学习、欢笑，和重新学会信任。这就是一个安全的地方能做到的事。

每个孩子都应该感到安全。

Somewhere to Call Home

For a very young child, home should be simple: a warm bed, a familiar face, someone who stays.

Where it began

Some of our youngest children spent their first years without a permanent place to stay, moving from place to place with a parent who was doing their best in impossible circumstances.

Coming home

Here, the little ones get what every small child needs: regular meals, naps in the same bed and caregivers who greet them by name each morning.

What comes next

Soon they will start school, make friends and learn what it feels like to be a child with a place to belong.

A child’s first need is a place to belong.

一个可以称之为家的地方

对年幼的孩子来说，家应该很简单：一张温暖的床、一张熟悉的脸，和一个不会离开的人。

故事的起点

我们最年幼的一些孩子，最初的几年没有固定的栖身之所，跟着尽了最大努力却处境艰难的家长，辗转于不同的地方。

来到小屋

在这里，小朋友得到每个幼儿都需要的东西：规律的三餐、在同一张床上午睡，以及每天早上叫得出他们名字的看护。

接下来

很快，他们将开始上学、结交朋友，并学会做一个有归属的孩子是什么感觉。

孩子最先需要的，是一个归属之地。
